Find the scale factor

A recipe gives ingredient amounts for a particular number of servings. To change the number of servings, multiply each ingredient amount by the same scale factor.

Scale factor = desired servings ÷ original servings.

Suppose a recipe serves 4 people and you want 6 servings. The scale factor is 6 ÷ 4 = 1.5. Each ingredient amount becomes one and a half times its original amount.

Apply the factor consistently

New amount = original amount × scale factor.

For this example, 200 g of rice becomes 300 g, and 400 mL of water becomes 600 mL.

IngredientOriginal amountMultiply byNew amount
Rice200 g1.5300 g
Water400 mL1.5600 mL

Multiplying both quantities by the same factor preserves their numerical proportion. The units do not change: grams remain grams and milliliters remain milliliters.

Know the boundary of the rule

This is a proportional arithmetic example, not a guarantee about cooking. Cooking time, pan size, and evaporation do not necessarily scale by the same factor. Follow the recipe’s guidance for those adjustments.

Pause and explain
